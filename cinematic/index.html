What this estimate includes — and what it doesn't
  • Your target is planned household spending in Australian dollars at today's prices. It is not a recommendation, a guaranteed income or a calculation of how much super you need.
  • Inputs are converted using 52 weeks, 26 fortnights and 12 months per year. Totals are calculated before rounding. Your target includes all category amounts, rent or mortgage payments, annualised big purchases, other allowances and any buffer.
  • The buffer applies to every category except travel, plus rent or mortgage payments. Future purchase allowances are purchase cost divided by years; no investment return or inflation is included in those allowances.
  • Starter figures are examples based on rounded monthly amounts displayed by the existing Hostplus Retirement Cost Calculator, inspected 17 October 2026. They are not the precise ASFA detailed budget and may sum differently from the benchmark. Housing category examples include household costs, not rent or mortgage repayments. Review rates, insurance and maintenance if renting.
  • ASFA benchmarks are annual household budgets for ages 65–84, June quarter 2026. Homeowner benchmarks assume a home owned outright. The modest renter benchmark is after Commonwealth Rent Assistance, while this tool includes your full rent. It is not a like-for-like comparison. No directly comparable mortgage benchmark is presented. Different housing and spending assumptions limit comparisons.
  • We don't calculate tax, Age Pension eligibility, investment returns, longevity, aged care, debt repayment timing or whether your savings can support this target. Before-tax income may need to be higher than planned spending.
  • Travel is already a category. Avoid adding the same holiday, home repairs or car costs in another allowance. Review costs as your circumstances change.
  • Future-dollar illustration = annual target × (1 + inflation rate) ^ years. Inflation is an editable assumption, not a forecast. The main target and comparison remain in today's dollars.
  • Inputs stay in this browser session unless you choose to save on this device. No inputs are sent to a server. Downloads contain your budget; keep them somewhere appropriate. A shared device may expose saved inputs to other users.
