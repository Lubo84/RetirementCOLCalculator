# Retirement Planner

[**Launch the live retirement planner →**](https://retirement-income-planner.lubo51.chatgpt.site)

A simple Australian retirement planner prototype that helps people connect the lifestyle they want with the income sources that may support it.

## What it helps estimate

- A weekly retirement living-cost target
- Superannuation available at retirement
- A possible government Age Pension alongside super
- The impact of clearing debt and retaining a safety buffer
- How long the selected income target may be maintained
- The effect of spending less, retiring later or contributing more
- One clear next action to improve the plan

The calculator uses today’s dollars and makes its assumptions and limitations visible throughout the journey.

## Important notice

This is a planning prototype and provides general information only. Results are estimates, not guarantees or personal financial advice. The Age Pension result is not an entitlement decision; Services Australia confirms eligibility and payment amounts. Consider obtaining advice from an Australian financial services licensee before making financial decisions.
